About Bimalism News

What we do, how we source our news, and what "curated" means on this site.

What Bimalism News is

Bimalism News is a live news aggregator. We pull real-time headlines from multiple licensed news APIs across eight sections — Breaking, National, International, Business, Technology, Sports, Entertainment, Health and Science — and organize them in one place so readers can scan what's happening across categories without hopping between a dozen different sites.

How sourcing works

Every headline, image and summary shown on the home page is pulled live from third-party news APIs and clearly attributed to its original publisher via a source tag on each card. Clicking "Read full article" takes you to the original publisher's website to read the full story — we do not republish full article text, and we do not claim authorship of aggregated headlines or summaries.

What "Editor's Picks" is

Alongside the live aggregator, our Editor's Picks section carries original writing produced by the Bimalism Desk: short explainers, context pieces and roundups written specifically for this site, not pulled from any external feed. That's where you'll find our own analysis and commentary rather than aggregated third-party content.

Corrections and takedowns

If a source attribution is wrong, an image is misattributed, or a publisher would like a headline or excerpt removed from our feed, contact us any time — see the Contact page — and we'll act on it promptly.
