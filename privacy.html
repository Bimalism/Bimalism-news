Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This policy explains what data bimalism.com collects, how it's used, and the third-party services embedded on this site.

Overview

Bimalism News ("we", "us", "this site") operates bimalism.com, a live news aggregator. This policy applies to everyone who visits bimalism.com.

Information stored in your browser

This site uses your browser's local storage (not a server database) to remember a few preferences on your device only:

  • Bookmarked articles you've saved
  • Your light/dark theme choice
  • A "broadcast" link if you've shared a specific story

This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and is cleared if you clear your browser storage.

Advertising — Google AdSense

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Partner Sites policy.

Other third-party services on this site

  • News content APIs — headlines, summaries, images and publish dates shown on the home page are retrieved live from third-party news aggregation APIs. We do not control how those providers handle data related to that retrieval.
  • Google Translate — the language selector on this site loads Google's Website Translator widget, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Algolia — search/autocomplete functionality on this site may be powered by Algolia, subject to Algolia's Privacy Policy.

Links to other websites

Articles on Bimalism News link out to the original publisher for the full story. We are not responsible for the content, accuracy, or privacy practices of external sites we link to.

Children's privacy

Bimalism News is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear bookmarks and preferences at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, and you can opt out of personalized ads using the links above. For any other request regarding your data, contact us at bimalism1@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: bimalism1@gmail.com.
